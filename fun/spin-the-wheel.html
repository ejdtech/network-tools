    About the Spin the Wheel tool

    Spin the Wheel is a free random name picker. You add names or options, one per line, and the wheel picks one of them at random. It needs no sign-up and no installation, and your list is never sent or saved.

    Ways to use it

    Pick who goes first in a stand-up, choose a presenter for the next meeting, decide where the team eats lunch, draw a winner for a small giveaway, pick a student to answer, or settle any "who will do it" question fairly.

    How to use it

    Type or paste your entries, one per line, or tap one of the quick lists. Click Spin the wheel (or click the wheel itself). Tick Remove the winner if you want to draw several different people in turn, for example for first, second and third prize.

    How the choice is made

    The winner is chosen first, using your browser's secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues) with no bias. The animation then slows down and stops on that winner. Every entry has the same chance, and a name entered twice has twice the chance.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the wheel really random?

    Yes. Each spin is an independent random choice. Earlier results do not change the next one, so the same name can win twice in a row unless you remove winners.

    How many entries can I add?

    Up to 60. Beyond that the labels become too small to read.

    Can I give some entries a better chance?

    Yes. Add the entry more than once. Two lines with the same name double its chances.

    Are my names stored anywhere?

    No. EJD Tech has no server-side code or database. The list exists only in the open page and disappears when you close or refresh it.

    Does it work on a phone?

    Yes. The wheel resizes to fit the screen, and you can tap it to spin.
