Check your wireless network information and signal strength.

Full signal-strength scanning isn't possible from a browser.

For privacy reasons, browsers don't give websites access to nearby Wi-Fi networks or signal strength (dBm) — that information is only available to native apps with OS-level permission. Above is the general connection info the browser is allowed to share.
