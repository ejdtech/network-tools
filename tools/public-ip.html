Find My Public IP

Shows the public IP address your browser is connecting from, plus rough location info. Nothing is saved — the lookup happens directly between your browser and the public API.

This tool calls two free public APIs directly from your browser: api.ipify.org (for the IP address) and ipapi.co (for location). ipapi.co's free tier is rate-limited — if the location lookup fails, the IP address will still be shown.
